Trigonometry

sin(x)

Section titled “sin(x)”

The sine of x, an angle in radians, or with a unit such as deg.

cos(x)

Section titled “cos(x)”

The cosine of x.

tan(x)

Section titled “tan(x)”

The tangent of x.

sec(x)

Section titled “sec(x)”

The secant of x, 1/cos(x).

csc(x)

Section titled “csc(x)”

The cosecant of x, 1/sin(x).

cot(x)

Section titled “cot(x)”

The cotangent of x, 1/tan(x).

asin(x)

Section titled “asin(x)”

The angle whose sine is x, between −π/2 and π/2.

acos(x)

Section titled “acos(x)”

The angle whose cosine is x, between 0 and π.

atan(x)

Section titled “atan(x)”

The angle whose tangent is x, between −π/2 and π/2.

atan2(x, y)

Section titled “atan2(x, y)”

The angle of the point (x, y) from the x axis, between −π and π; x and y may have units, the same.

acot(x)

Section titled “acot(x)”

The angle whose cotangent is x, between 0 and π.

asec(x)

Section titled “asec(x)”

The angle whose secant is x.

acsc(x)

Section titled “acsc(x)”

The angle whose cosecant is x.

sinh(x)

Section titled “sinh(x)”

The hyperbolic sine of x.

cosh(x)

Section titled “cosh(x)”

The hyperbolic cosine of x.

tanh(x)

Section titled “tanh(x)”

The hyperbolic tangent of x.

sech(x)

Section titled “sech(x)”

The hyperbolic secant of x, 1/cosh(x).

csch(x)

Section titled “csch(x)”

The hyperbolic cosecant of x, 1/sinh(x).

coth(x)

Section titled “coth(x)”

The hyperbolic cotangent of x, 1/tanh(x).

asinh(x)

Section titled “asinh(x)”

The inverse hyperbolic sine of x.

acosh(x)

Section titled “acosh(x)”

The inverse hyperbolic cosine of x, for x of 1 or more.

atanh(x)

Section titled “atanh(x)”

The inverse hyperbolic tangent of x, for x between −1 and 1.

acoth(x)

Section titled “acoth(x)”

The inverse hyperbolic cotangent of x, for x beyond −1 and 1.
