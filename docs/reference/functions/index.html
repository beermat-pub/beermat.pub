Functions

The built-in functions, by group:

  • Numbers and rounding
  • Trigonometry
  • Arrays
  • Sums, calculus and solving
  • Statistics and data
  • Strings, units and programs

And all of them from A to Z:

Function What it does
abs The absolute value of x, or the magnitude of a complex number. Also written |x|.
acos The angle whose cosine is x, between 0 and π.
acosh The inverse hyperbolic cosine of x, for x of 1 or more.
acot The angle whose cotangent is x, between 0 and π.
acoth The inverse hyperbolic cotangent of x, for x beyond −1 and 1.
acsc The angle whose cosecant is x.
arg The angle of z in the complex plane, between −π and π.
asec The angle whose secant is x.
asin The angle whose sine is x, between −π/2 and π/2.
asinh The inverse hyperbolic sine of x.
atan The angle whose tangent is x, between −π/2 and π/2.
atan2 The angle of the point (x, y) from the x axis, between −π and π; x and y may have units, the same.
atanh The inverse hyperbolic tangent of x, for x between −1 and 1.
augment The matrices side by side; they need the same number of rows.
Ceil The smallest multiple of y not below x.
ceil The smallest integer not below x.
cells A range of a named table’s grid, written as in a spreadsheet, the header being row 1.
CFFT As cfft, scaled by 1/n and with the opposite sign in the exponent.
cfft The Fourier transform of a complex vector or matrix of any size, scaled by 1/√n.
cnorm The probability that a standard normal variable is below x.
cols The number of columns of A.
concat The strings joined.
conj The complex conjugate of z, element by element in an array.
corr The correlation coefficient of the two vectors.
cos The cosine of x.
cosh The hyperbolic cosine of x.
cot The cotangent of x, 1/tan(x).
coth The hyperbolic cotangent of x, 1/tanh(x).
cross The cross product of the three-element vectors u and v.
csc The cosecant of x, 1/sin(x).
csch The hyperbolic cosecant of x, 1/sinh(x).
csort The rows of A sorted by column j.
cspline A cubic spline through the points, with cubic ends, for interp.
diff The derivative of f with respect to x (the nth derivative, up to 5) at x’s current value.
dnorm The probability density of the normal distribution at x.
eigenvals The eigenvalues of the square matrix M, as a column.
eigenvec The eigenvector of M for the eigenvalue λ, of length 1.
eigenvecs The eigenvectors of M, one per column, in the order of eigenvals(M), each of length 1.
erf The error function.
erfc The complementary error function, 1 − erf(x).
error Stops with the message s: in a program, for a value that can’t be used.
exp e to the power x.
FFT As fft, scaled by 1/n and with the opposite sign in the exponent.
fft The Fourier transform of the real vector v (2ⁿ elements), its first half and the middle, scaled by 1/√n.
find Below given, the values of the unknowns that satisfy its constraints, starting from their current values.
Floor The largest multiple of y not above x; x and y may have units, the same.
floor The largest integer not above x.
gamma The gamma function, as Γ(x).
hist How many of the data fall in each interval: intervals is a vector of their ends, or the number of equal intervals.
histogram As hist, beside the middle of each interval: a column of middles, then one of counts.
hlookup The elements of row i of A in the columns whose first element is z.
ICFFT The inverse of CFFT.
icfft The inverse of cfft.
identity The n×n identity matrix.
if a if c is true (not zero), otherwise b; only the one chosen is evaluated.
IFFT The inverse of FFT.
ifft The inverse of fft: the real vector whose transform is c.
Im The imaginary part of z.
int The definite integral of f over x from a to b; either limit may be ∞.
intercept Where the least-squares straight line crosses x = 0.
interp The value at x of a spline from cspline, pspline or lspline, or of a polynomial from regress.
IsArray 1 if x is an array, 0 if not.
IsScalar 1 if x is a number, 0 if not.
IsString 1 if x is a string, 0 if not.
last The index of the last element of v, counting from ORIGIN.
length The number of elements of the vector v.
linfit The least-squares coefficients of a sum of terms: F(x) returns a vector of the terms.
linspace n values evenly spaced from a to b, as a column.
linterp The value at x of the straight lines between the points (vx, vy); beyond the ends, the end line carries on.
ln The natural logarithm of x.
lnΓ The natural logarithm of the gamma function, for large x.
log The logarithm of x to base 10, or to base b.
logspace n values from a to b, evenly spaced on a log scale.
lookup The elements of B in the places where A holds z.
lspline A cubic spline with straight ends (the natural spline), for interp.
match The positions in A of the value z, or with mode “near”, “lt”, “gt”, “leq” or “geq” of the nearest value, the one just below or just above.
matrix An m×n matrix whose element (i, j) is f(i, j), i and j counting from 0.
max The largest of the numbers given, arrays included element by element.
Maximize The values of the variables where f(x, y, …) is greatest.
mean The mean of all the numbers given.
median The median of all the numbers given.
min The smallest of the numbers given.
minerr Below given, the values that come closest to satisfying its constraints, when they can’t all hold.
Minimize The values of the variables, from their current values, where f(x, y, …) is least; below given, within its constraints.
mod The remainder of x divided by y, with the sign of x.
nthroot The real nth root of x; an odd root of a negative number is negative.
num2str The number x as a string.
Odesolve Below given, the solution of its differential equation and initial conditions as a function of t, up to b.
pause Returns x, for compatibility with imported sheets.
pnorm The probability that a normal variable is below x.
polyroots The roots of the polynomial v₀ + v₁x + v₂x² + …, as a column, complex where they are.
prod The product of the elements of v, or of f over i. Also written with Π.
pspline A cubic spline with parabolic ends, for interp.
qnorm The x below which a normal variable falls with probability p.
Re The real part of z.
READCSV A comma-separated file as a matrix, numbers and text kept apart.
READEXCEL The cells of an Excel workbook (.xlsx or .xls), all of its first sheet or a range; in the desktop app.
READFILE A data file, type being “delimited” (spaces, tabs or commas) or “csv”.
READPRN The numbers in a text file, in columns separated by spaces, tabs or commas; lines that aren’t numbers are skipped.
regress The least-squares polynomial of degree k through the points, for interp.
reverse The elements of v in reverse order (the rows of a matrix).
Rkadapt As rkfixed, at the same n points, with adaptive steps between them for accuracy.
rkadapt The same as Rkadapt.
rkfixed The solution of y’ = D(x, y) from x1 to x2 in n equal steps (fourth-order Runge–Kutta): a column of x, then one per element of y.
rnd A random number between 0 and x, the same at every recalculation.
rnorm m random numbers from the normal distribution, as a column.
root A value of x where f is zero: from x’s current value as a guess, or between a and b where f changes sign.
Round x rounded to the nearest multiple of y.
round x rounded to n decimal places, or to an integer.
rows The number of rows of A (0 for a number).
rsort The columns of A sorted by row i.
runif m random numbers between a and b, as a column.
search The position of t in s, looking from position m, or −1 if it isn’t there.
sec The secant of x, 1/cos(x).
sech The hyperbolic secant of x, 1/cosh(x).
sign −1, 0 or 1, as x is negative, zero or positive.
signum z divided by its magnitude, or 0 when z is 0.
sin The sine of x, an angle in radians, or with a unit such as deg.
sinh The hyperbolic sine of x.
SIUnitsOf One of x’s unit in SI, so that x/SIUnitsOf(x) is the plain number.
slope The slope of the least-squares straight line through the points.
sort The elements of v in ascending order.
sqrt The square root of x, with its units halved.
stack The matrices one above the other; they need the same number of columns.
Stdev The standard deviation of a sample.
stdev The standard deviation of a whole population.
str2num The number written in the string s.
strlen The number of characters in s.
submatrix The part of A from row ir to jr and column ic to jc, counting from ORIGIN.
substr n characters of s from position m (counting from 0).
sum The sum of the elements of v, or of f over the range variable i, or over i from a to b. Also written with Σ.
tan The tangent of x.
tanh The hyperbolic tangent of x.
time The time now in seconds (z is not used), for timing a long calculation.
tr The trace of the square matrix M, the sum of its diagonal.
trace Returns x, for compatibility with imported sheets.
Trunc The multiple of y nearest x towards zero.
trunc The integer part of x, towards zero.
UnitsOf The same as SIUnitsOf.
use The functions of a Python file, as a module: bt := use "beam.py", then bt.f(x); in the desktop app.
Var The variance of the numbers as a sample (dividing by n − 1).
var The variance of the numbers as a whole population (dividing by n).
vec The expression evaluated element by element over its arrays (the vectorize arrow).
vlookup The elements of column j of A in the rows whose first element is z.
Γ The gamma function: Γ(n) is (n − 1)! for a whole number n.
